Snell Spear privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Snell Spear is a game for Android published by Mushahid Hussain. This policy explains what the game does with information about you.

Information we collect

None. The game does not collect, transmit or share any personal or device information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it does not request permission to use the internet.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your best scores, your Daily Dive result, your chosen location, whether you have finished the tutorial and your sound setting. This stays in the game's private storage on your device and is never sent to us.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this data in your device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us.

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes this data.

Children

Because the game collects no information, it collects no information from children.

Changes

If a future version changes what the game does with information, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
